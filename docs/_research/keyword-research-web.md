# Keyword research for an Islamic-first coloring and activity site (public sources only)

**How I got the numbers.** The page-fetch tool failed with a DNS error on every domain, including Google Trends, RankHero, Ahrefs and Wikipedia. Running curl against Google autocomplete was denied by the permission system. Every figure below therefore comes from web-search snippets of public Semrush, Ahrefs, RankHero and indexator.ai pages.

The search engine I used is not Google, so the "who ranks" notes are only a proxy for Google results. I found no published volume for any Islamic coloring keyword. Anything marked **(est.)** is my own calibrated guess, not a sourced number. Before committing content, check these in Google Keyword Planner (free with an Ads account) and Google Trends. In Trends, compare each term against "dinosaur coloring pages", which RankHero puts at 74K a month, to turn the index into rough volumes.

## D. Seasonal calendar

**General coloring searches:**
- Overall coloring demand is lowest in July ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/coloring-pages)) and highest Nov–Mar.
- Back to school: late Jul–early Sep
- Fall: Sep–Nov, peak Oct
- Halloween: rises early Sep, peaks the last two weeks of Oct
- Thanksgiving: November
- Christmas: rises in Nov, peaks the first three weeks of Dec
- Winter: Dec–Feb
- Valentine's: late Jan–Feb 14
- St Patrick's: late Feb–Mar 17
- Spring: Mar–Apr
- Easter: the two to three weeks before (Mar 28, 2027; Apr 16, 2028)
- Mother's Day: US late Apr–May, UK March
- Father's Day: June
- Summer: May–Jul

These general timings are standard patterns, not sourced figures.

**Islamic calendar.** Dates move about 11 days earlier each Gregorian year and can shift ±1 day with moon sighting.

| Event | 2026 (reference) | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ramadan start | ~Feb 18 | **~Feb 8** | **~Jan 28** |
| Eid al-Fitr | ~Mar 20 | **~Mar 9–10** | **~Feb 26–27** |
| Eid al-Adha | ~May 27 | **~May 16** | **~May 5** |
| Islamic New Year | ~Jun 16 | ~Jun 5–6 | ~May 24–25 |
| Mawlid (12 Rabi' al-Awwal) | ~Aug 25 | ~Aug 14 | ~Aug 3 |

Sources: [HRF](https://www.hrf.org.uk/media-centre/blog/when-is-ramadan-2027/), [Wusul](https://wusul.io/calendar/), [Calendarific 2028](https://calendarific.com/holidays/2028/sa), [islamic.app](https://islamic.app/hijri/mawlid/2027), [TrueCalendar](https://truecalendar.com/c/10433c6e320438108454b6d102251118/2028). The 2026 column is from my own knowledge.

**Timing for publishing:**
- "ramadan" averages 1.5M searches a month but peaked at 7.48M in Feb 2026, roughly 5× ([RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/ramadan)).
- Google says Ramadan shoppers start planning up to 8 weeks ahead ([Google](https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/diversity/4-ways-google-can-you-help-you-celebrate-ramadan/)).
- In Indonesia, Ramadan searches start rising about a week before fasting begins ([Webcertain](https://webcertain.com/site/knowhowamp/google-releases-ramadan-search-data/kb1987)).
- Plan: publish 6–8 weeks before, pin to Pinterest 4–6 weeks before.
- Eid al-Fitr pages peak in the last 10 days of Ramadan. Eid al-Adha and Hajj pages peak in the first 10 days of Dhul Hijjah.
- Mawlid matters in Indonesia, Malaysia, Egypt and South Asia (school coloring contests, e.g. [HaiBunda](https://haibunda.com/parenting/20260819124030-61-396257/7-gambar-mewarnai-tema-maulid-nabi-untuk-lomba-anak-anak)), but some families don't observe it. Keep it as a separate, optional section.
- In 2028 Ramadan starts in January, and around 2030 it reaches late December, so Ramadan will overlap the peak months for general coloring traffic.

### Content conventions

Rulings range from prohibition ([IslamQA 315944](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/315944)), to allowing children's drawings without facial features ([IslamQA 87720](https://islamqa.info/en/answers/87720)), to permission for educational use while avoiding defined faces ([Hanafi, MuftiOnline](https://islamqa.org/hanafi/muftionline/114713/using-animate-pictures-for-educational-purposes/)). Islamic children's books have relaxed these rules since the late 1990s ([Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/c74806f4-f461-4864-a135-54c5cb7a2375)). Recommended rules:

- **Never depict** prophets, angels or Allah, even though some competitor sets include "angels" and "prophets" pages.
- **Default subjects:** architecture, geometric patterns, calligraphy, nature, lanterns, crescent, food, objects.
- **Animals:** mark them as a separate filter.
- **People:** faceless only, with modest dress.
- **Add a site-wide "no faces" toggle.** No competitor offers one, so it would be a clear differentiator.

## F. Search-results observations

- **Hubs win over single pages.** Coloringlover gets about 180K visits from roughly 95 pages ([Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/websites/coloring.ws)).
- **Traffic is spread thin.** Supercoloring's top keyword brings in under 1% of its traffic ([Semrush](https://de.semrush.com/website/supercoloring.com/overview)), so breadth of long-tail coverage beats chasing head terms.
- **Image rich results matter.** Monday Mandala's top keywords all show image rich results, and its traffic grew from 82K to 578K in 4 months ([AIOSEO](https://aioseo.com/trends/monday-mandala-seo-case-study/)). Use ImageObject schema, descriptive file names and alt text.
- **One PDF per secondary keyword.** A 7-month-old site using this method is estimated at $3–5K a month ([Niche Site Growth](https://nichesitegrowth.beehiiv.com/p/these-niche-sites-are-flying)).
- **AI risk.** Since Jul 14, 2026, Google can generate images inside AI Overviews (Nano Banana), and Google Images was redesigned into a Pinterest-like personalised gallery ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-brings-image-generation-into-ai-overviews-as-google-images-turns-25/), [DesignCompass](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/07/16/google-images-25-for-you-redesign/)). Simple "[x] coloring page" queries are exposed. Bundled PDFs, worksheets and curriculum-style sets are much harder to replace.
- **Traffic mix.** Google organic is 51–66% and direct 24–36% at the big sites. Supercoloring already receives about 4.8K visits a month from ChatGPT. I found no coloring-specific Pinterest data.
- **Pinterest matters most in Indonesia.** Indonesian publishers send readers to Pinterest to download images, so Pinterest acts as the file host there. Treat it as the primary seasonal channel for Ramadan and Eid.

## G. Emerging trends, 2025–2026

- **AI generators:**
  - "ai coloring page generator": 4,370 a month, later 2,450 ([indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/ai-coloring-pages))
  - "coloring page generator": 850–1,380
  - "image to coloring page converter": 430 ([indexator](https://indexator.ai/companies/colorme-ai))
  - "photo to coloring page": est. 1–3K
  - ChatGPT is commonly used to turn photos into coloring pages ([Frugal Fun 4 Boys](https://frugalfun4boys.com/use-chatgpt-to-make-custom-coloring-pages/)). Possible feature: "turn your masjid or Eid photo into a coloring page".
- **Bold and easy / cozy:**
  - Nearly half of Amazon's adult coloring bestsellers in Oct 2025 were bold-and-easy (a CNN observation).
  - US children's coloring and activity print sales hit about 16.7M units in 2025, up 18% (Circana) ([BookCoversLab](https://bookcoverslab.com/blog/best-kdp-niches-2026)).
  - The style traces back to Meg Miles and was being discussed on r/coloring by 2024 ([Book Riot](https://bookriot.com/bold-and-cozy-coloring-coco-wyo-controversies/)).
  - Est. 20–50K a month and rising. It fits "bold and easy Ramadan" pages for little children.
- **Licensed fads (IP risk, excluded):**
  - Labubu: searches up 9,800% year over year in Jun 2025, peaked Jul–Aug 2025, now cooling ([Trendalytics](https://trendalytics.co/news/the-rise-of-ugly-cute-labubu-dolls-in-four-charts))
  - KPop Demon Hunters: among the top US Google searches of 2025 ([TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2025/12/04/kpop-demon-hunters-top-google-searches/))
  - Stitch: boosted by the May 2025 live-action film ([D23](https://d23.com/downloadable-celebrate-lilo-stitch-with-this-fun-activity-pack/))
  - Peak demand for coloring versions of each is likely 100K+ a month (est.).

**Bottom line.** English Islamic coloring demand is modest (est. 30–60K a month on average across all terms, with about 5× peaks around Ramadan). The two things worth building around are the spam-filled Islamic-education results and Indonesian/Arabic demand that nobody serves well. Islamic-filtered evergreen categories (Quran animals, mosques, Arabic letters, geometric patterns) can carry traffic outside the season.
